# Public-data next-step decision

The completed IntelliRehabDS SSL adapter pilot did not improve the strict
ten-fold product LOSO result (`-0.0346` paired Macro-F1 versus Optimized-v1),
so no additional blind train is authorized.

The remaining public stroke-functional-reaching source has roughly 30 subjects
and many workbooks, but it stores proprietary-format reaching trajectories, not
the product's 33-joint MediaPipe skeleton. Its movement names and completion
scores are not the four RehabAI product labels. It therefore remains auxiliary
until a trajectory-only adapter is explicitly designed and passes the same
strict gate.

The immediate quality-improving priority is new, balanced, product-domain data:
each new subject should perform all four product exercises with repeated
successful and unsuccessful trials. Existing production and Optimized-v1 remain
unchanged.
